Hey everybody, it's Friday, May 1st. Rob Booker here. Welcome to your Longboard business update.

## New Homepage

I've changed the homepage of longboardai.com, and you will now see what I think is a beautiful homepage that invites people to get the free email of the day. That email is one of the main ways we are going to grow the list.

It took a surprisingly longer amount of time than I thought it would. But the good news is we now have **four variants** of that page that swap out the headline, the imagery, the promise, or the offer. That gives us real material to test against, and I think we are going to be really happy with the results.

The bigger point: we now have a place for people to land when I start running ads on social media. That was the prerequisite for everything else.

The New Traders Podcast feed is fixed. New episodes are now showing up in Spotify and Apple Podcasts. Go over there and check them out. I think you are going to love what we are doing.
