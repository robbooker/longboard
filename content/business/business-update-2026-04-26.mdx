## Advertising and Lead Generation

I have reactivated my Facebook account so we can begin running ads again. This was not my dream recreational activity, but here we are. My goal is to make our financial publishing ads more interesting than the usual ads in this space, which is admittedly a low bar but still one worth clearing.

We are going to test several angles, including:

- An artificial intelligence stock scanner
- A stock-of-the-day style offer
- A relaunch of my short book, *Trading by Faith*

*Trading by Faith* is a short book for traders who are people of faith. It does not try to merge trading and religion, which would be a bad idea for several obvious reasons and a few compliance-related ones. The basic message is simple: if you believe there are things more important than trading, and things more important than money, you are already ahead of most people.

I expect to begin recording ads near the end of this week, though it may take seven to ten days to get everything fully ready. I am working on lining up an affordable video team, getting the teleprompter set up again, and choosing three or four nearby shooting locations. Once that is in place, we should be able to record 50 to 100 ads and begin testing them.

For May, I have a $50,000 advertising budget. The target is to generate at least 5,000 new names and email addresses for our list. If we can consistently add 5,000 names per month, that could potentially support $200,000 to $250,000 per month in gross revenue over time.

Of course, ads may be expensive. They may not work. Facebook may decide it does not enjoy our particular form of enthusiasm. We may get shut down and have to come back up again, which is basically the unofficial sport of running ads online. Still, this is one of the most exciting parts of what we are building.

If you have experience running ads, please feel free to get in touch with me. I do not need anyone to jump in yet, but I would like to know who has relevant experience before the machine starts making machine noises.

## One-Page Business Plan

This week, I am also writing a one-page business plan.

The business plan is intentionally simple:

1. Run ads on Facebook, Instagram, and YouTube.
2. Ask people for their email address.
3. Follow up immediately with offers.

That is the core plan. There are fancier ways to say this, and consultants have built entire vacation homes by saying them, but the business is fundamentally list growth plus thoughtful offers.

Once we have the business structure in place and the website set up to help people find, trade, and manage trades, my focus will be almost entirely on growth.

That means:

- Running paid ads on social media
- Building partnerships with other trading educators
- Growing the email list
- Sending free offers, low-cost offers, webinar invitations, and Boardroom offers

If I am spending time on anything else, it needs to clearly support that mission.

## Website Value Proposition

The website needs to communicate a simple and compelling value proposition:

We help you find, trade, and manage stocks that are worth buying and holding for a few minutes to a few hours.

That is the core of what we are building. The website does not need to become a museum of features. It needs to make the promise clear, deliver on that promise, and help us turn attention into relationships and relationships into revenue.

Once that foundation is live, I will focus on running as many ads as possible, as affordably as possible. If ads do not work the way we want, I will shift more energy into partnerships with other trading educators and other ways to grow the list.
