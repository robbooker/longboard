## Why The Video Provider Matters

Longboardai.com is going to have a lot of video. Most of the courses will be free. The dashboard will look more like Netflix than like a typical course site, and eventually it will move to a $39/month public subscription. Boardroom members get it free.

The model I want to operate is closer to the Tony Robbins network — an ongoing stream of programming pushed out all day long, with short breaks for promotional spots. Once we have done two or three of our live events together, we will have hours of well-produced footage of me talking to traders and teaching, and we can pair that with the thousands of hours of video I have made over the years to create a continuous stream.

That stream is what brings new people in. We can also republish the same content on YouTube to grow the channel. Everything is in service of one thing: discover people, give them something useful, and ask for their email address.

To make this work economically, we need a video provider that does not bankrupt us. Hosting hundreds of videos that play for thousands of hours a day gets expensive in a hurry. I think I have found one — MUX — that makes the math work. Final decision soon.
