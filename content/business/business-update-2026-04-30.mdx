## The Contest

Most likely starting next Wednesday and running for a week, we are going to launch a daily contest.

Here is the shape of it:

Traders will be invited to input their email address and become a free Longboard AI member. In exchange, every day they will get to submit their pick for the top stock of the day, plus the price they think it will reach and the reasoning behind it. Submissions have to be in before the pre-market closes.

**We will give away $1,000 a day.**

I think we are going to get into the habit of running skills-oriented contests like this, because they are exciting and they actually work. When TradingView runs their big paper trading contests and gives away tens of thousands of dollars in prizes, they get a ton of interest and they bring people back to the website. We are going to do something in that spirit.

The philosophy is simple: give, give, give.

I am setting aside part of the marketing budget for this. **Rich Schaff**, one of our members, is going to help us with the development of the contest infrastructure.
