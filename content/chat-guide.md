# A guide to the Rob Booker chat

The chat is at chat.robbooker.com/chat. It has rooms (shared with other members), direct messages (DMs, private between two people), and a few pages such as the FAQ.

## Getting around

### On a computer
The list of rooms and DMs is the sidebar on the left. From top to bottom it shows any conversations you have pinned, What's New?, the FAQ, Rooms, Announcements, Recordings, Pages, a Search button, and Direct messages. You only see the rooms your membership includes.

Headings such as Rooms and Direct messages can be folded so only the heading shows. Folding is remembered on that device.

At the top of an open room or DM are Latest (jump to the newest messages), Search chat, an info button (i), the notification bell and the ••• menu.

Press Ctrl+K (Cmd+K on a Mac) to open the quick switcher: type a few letters of a room or a person and press Enter.

### On a phone
On a phone the list of rooms and DMs is its own home screen, with Search near the top and the bell and ••• menu in its top bar. Tap a room or DM to open it, and use the back arrow to return to the list.

Inside a room or DM, tap the info button (i) or the name at the top to open its info sheet. On a phone, the room's own options (pin, favorite, notifications, search) are in this sheet.

The phone layout is used on narrow touch screens. A tablet or a wide window uses the computer layout.

### Installing on a phone
On iPhone, open chat.robbooker.com/chat in Safari, sign in, tap Share, choose Add to Home Screen and tap Add. On Android, open it in Chrome, sign in, tap Chrome's ⋮ menu and choose Install and create shortcut, then Install. The wording can differ by browser version. The app appears as Rob Booker Chat.

## Rooms and what each one is for

Which rooms you see depends on your membership.

### LB
The Longboard room, for trading and the markets. For Longboard members in cohort 1 or 2. Members can post and reply.

### Social
Movies, life and everything else. Shown as "Social (LB + SS)" because it is open to all Longboard and ShortScout members. Members can post and reply.

### SS
The ShortScout room, for short selling. For ShortScout mastermind members. Members can post and reply.

### Gainers
A read-only channel of live alerts about stocks that are moving, posted automatically. An alert looks like: BRZE +52.60% · Pre-Market. Nobody can post or reply here, including admins, but you can react. It is open to all Longboard and ShortScout members.

If you have phone notifications on, you can turn on "Gainers alerts on this device" to get a notification for every new post. Busy mornings can bring dozens. On a computer, Gainers can also be popped out into its own window with the Pop out button.

### LB Review
Each trading day's gainers, charted. The day's gainers page is posted here in the evening. It is open to all chat members, and members can post and reply.

### Developers
For APIs, web pages, dashboards and apps: share what you are building. It is open to all chat members, and members can post and reply.

### What's New?
Updates to the chat, posted as they go live. Only admins and the automatic updates post here. You can react, but there are no replies.

### LB Announcements
Announcements for Longboard members in cohort 1 or 2. Only admins post. New announcements also appear in your notification bell.

### SS Announcements
Announcements for ShortScout mastermind members. Only admins post. New announcements also appear in your notification bell.

### LB Recordings
NEEDS ADMIN: what is posted here. The code only says it holds recordings, that admins post, that readers can react but not reply, and that it is for Longboard members in cohort 1 or 2.

### SS Recordings
NEEDS ADMIN: what is posted here. The code only says it holds recordings, that admins post, that readers can react but not reply, and that it is for ShortScout mastermind members.

### Admins
A private room only admins can see. FAQ suggestions from members arrive here.

## Direct messages

To start one, choose ＋ Start New DM above your list of DMs, type at least two letters of the person's name, and choose them from the list. On a phone the list is on the home screen.

Your first message to someone is a request: you can send one message, and you can keep chatting after they accept. When someone sends you a request, you can accept, decline or block.

To stop new requests, or to change DM sounds, open the ••• menu and choose DM settings.

For one conversation, open it and use the info button (i). There you can pin it, make it your favorite, change its sound or mute it, block, or report.

## Pinning

There are two different kinds of pin.

### Pin a room or DM to your sidebar
This keeps a room or DM at the top of your own list. Only you see your pins, and they follow your account to your other devices. You can pin up to 50.

To pin a room on a computer: open the room, click the ••• menu at the top right, and choose Pin followed by the room's name. You can also click the info button (i) and choose it there.

To pin a room on a phone: open the room, tap the info button (i) or the room's name at the top, and tap Pin followed by the room's name.

To pin a DM on a computer: open the conversation, click the info button (i) or the person's name at the top, and choose Pin followed by their name. The ••• menu also gets you there through DM settings.

To pin a DM on a phone: open the conversation, tap the info button (i) or the person's name at the top, and tap Pin followed by their name.

You can only pin a DM after the request has been accepted, and not while you have the person blocked.

Pinned conversations appear in a Pinned section at the top of your list. That section only shows once you have a pin.

To unpin: press the × beside it in the Pinned section, or go back to the same place you pinned it and choose Unpin.

### Pin a message in a room
Admins can pin a message so everyone in the room can see it, using Pin message in the message's ••• menu. Members can't pin messages. Each room can have up to 10 pinned messages. Open the room's info button (i) and look under Pinned to see them, and choose one to jump to it.

## Replies and threads

To reply to a message, choose Reply under it. That starts a conversation under that message without filling up the room. On a touch screen you can also swipe right on the message, or use its ••• menu.

On a large screen the replies open in a side panel next to the room. On a smaller screen they open on their own screen; use Back or Close to return.

There are no replies in Gainers, Recordings or What's New?. In announcement rooms, only admins can start replies.

## Reactions

Choose Add reaction on a message, then Like, Heart, Laughing or Rob. It appears when you hover over a message on a computer. Choose a reaction already showing to add yours, and choose it again to take yours off. To see everyone who reacted, press and hold it on a touch screen, or press Shift+F10 on a keyboard. You can react in DMs too.

## Search

On a computer, use Search in the sidebar or Search chat at the top of a room. On a phone, use Search on the home screen, or Search in the room from a room's info sheet.

Choose where to look (for example LB, Social or Developers) and how: Meaning + words finds related messages as well as matching ones, and Words & phrases looks for what you typed. Choosing a result opens the message in its conversation.

Search covers conversation rooms you can read: LB, Social, SS, Developers and LB Review. It doesn't cover Gainers, announcements, recordings, What's New? or DMs.

## Notifications

The bell at the top shows your notifications. For each room you can pick All messages, Mentions only or Muted, from the ••• menu on a computer or the info sheet on a phone.

For phone notifications, install the chat on your phone, open the ••• menu, choose Phone notifications, tap Enable notifications, allow them when your phone asks, then tap Send test notification to check it works.

## Favorite

You can keep one favorite room or DM as a shortcut in the ••• menu. Choosing a new one replaces the old one. It is separate from pins.

## The FAQ and suggesting a question

The FAQ is in the sidebar under What's New?. You can search it, and use Suggest a topic to send in a question. Admins review each suggestion before it appears.
