The ten rules

Tape these to your monitor.

  1. Close is close enough.A small loss on a trade you've already judged badly is a gift. Take it.
  2. You don't need every trade to become a win.Managing a bad trade well is the win.
  3. Shame destroys traders. Losses don't.Feel bad about the loss. Never about who you are.
  4. Confident without arrogance.You cannot trade well believing you are bad, broken, or undeserving.
  5. Regret is not a trading plan."I should have held it" — stop it. Good enough is amazing.
  6. You are going to do it again.So reduce your trade size before it happens, not after.
  7. They are the same as you.When someone blows up, learn from it. Next time it could be your turn.
  8. The worse it gets, the worse we act.Build the guardrails before the crisis.
  9. The system is not the problem.If it were enough, everyone in the room would already be rich.
  10. Stop saying sorry. Change.Say it out loud: "I'm in a bad trade." "I'm not doing well today."
