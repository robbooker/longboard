The RVOL signal, filtered.

A momentum-day catcher built around the E. Phillips RVOL signal, run on a clean 542-event April gap-up universe. After three small filters, win rate climbs from 38% to 48%, profit factor from 1.39 to 2.01. This page documents what the strategy does, what it caught, and what it missed.

MAY 03, 2026 · 5-minute bars, $1,000 per trade · 45 raw signals → 29 trades

01How the strategy works

A trade starts when six gates align on a single 5-minute bar: price between $1 and $20, relative volume at least 5× the prior 100 minutes, the bar closing above session VWAP and the 9-EMA, breaking the premarket high, with the prior bar red or below the 9-EMA, and the current close above the prior high. Entry happens at that bar's close. From there, a stop ladder takes over.

Entry rules — all six must be true

  1. Price $1.00 – $20.00
  2. RVOL ≥ 5 (lookback 20 bars)
  3. Close above session VWAP
  4. Close above 9-EMA
  5. High breaks the premarket high
  6. Prior bar red, OR closed below 9-EMA

v3 filters — added this month

  1. RVOL at entry must be ≥ 7 (kills the noise band)
  2. No entries during lunch 12:00–14:00 ET
  3. No entries in the closing 30 minutes ≥ 15:30 ET
  4. Hold position to 20:00 ET if not stopped out

02The stop ladder

One trade lives in three phases. The stop tightens as the trade works in our favor; it never loosens.

Stop progression as price moves from entry

A trade enters at the green dot. From entry, the position is risked to −20%. If price reaches +50%, the stop ratchets up to breakeven; the trade can no longer lose money. If price reaches +100%, the rule switches to a 20% trailing stop off the high — meaning the stop now ratchets up with every new high. EOD force-close at 20:00 ET if no stop fires.
