§ Lead · Trade of the day

The MARA short that almost wasn't.

A clean setup, a sloppy entry, and a thirty-second window where the right answer was to do nothing at all. Notes from the morning, with the chart and the audio.

Rob Booker · 6 min read · 12 min watch · tape attached
▶ Watch 12:04 · MARA tape, 9:31–9:43 ET

MARA, 1-min, April 15. Halt at 22.40 (red), reclaim into the 5-min VWAP (green). Bracket: short 22.10, cover 19.80.

The setup was the boring one. Parabolic move into a known halt level, then the LUDP halt, then the question every short trader asks themselves at 9:31 in the morning: is this the one where I get my face ripped off, or the one I tell my friends about for a week.

The answer, as it usually is, was neither. It was a 3.4R trade and I almost didn't take it because I'd already lost on OPRA the day before and my hands were doing that thing where they hover over the keyboard but don't actually press anything. I want to talk about the hands.
