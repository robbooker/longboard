# Black Swan

**Status:** planned — not live, not funded, not scheduled.
**Needs:** options enabled on a paper account; VIX-adjacent ETF universe (UVXY, VXX, VIXY, UVIX) vetted for liquidity on the option chain.

## Mandate

Continuously long volatility via UVXY-type calls.

## Why it's parked

This strategy runs counter to the existing `forbidden everywhere` rule on the Long/Short Portfolio (no leveraged ETFs, no inverse/short ETFs, no options). Black Swan needs a separate carve-out in its own spec because its *thesis* is "buy the thing the other strategy refuses to touch." The carve-out has to be designed, not retrofitted.

Full spec lands the week of Phase 2 ship or later.
